# Customer Guide 1: Using the Hellobee Store

How to find products, order, pay, and follow your order on the Hellobee website (`https://www.hellobee.org`).

## 1. Create an account (optional)

You can order as a guest, but an account saves your details, shipping addresses, and order history.

1. Select the account icon in the top bar, then **Register New Account**.
2. Enter your full name, email, US phone number, and a password (minimum 8 characters).
3. If a Hellobee consultant referred you, enter their code in the sponsor field (for example `HBEE03`). It is saved to your
   account permanently, so your consultant is credited on every purchase even if you forget to type it later. You pay nothing extra.
4. Sign in with your email and password. If you forget your password, choose **Forgot password?** and follow the email link.

Phone numbers use US format: `(555) 123-4567` or `+1 (555) 123-4567`.

## 2. Find a product

- **Shop** in the top menu lists every product. Use the filters for category, electrode count, and price, plus the sort menu.
- **Search** (magnifier icon, or `Cmd/Ctrl + K`) finds products by name or SKU.
- Open a product to see photos, finish/color options, package configurations, description, specifications, warranty, and reviews.
- Select the heart icon to save or remove an item in your **Wishlist** (`/wishlist`).

## 3. Add to cart

1. Select **Order Now** on a product card, or open the product page.
2. Choose the finish/color and configuration package.
3. Select **Add to Cart**. The cart drawer opens with your items and subtotal.
4. Change quantities or remove items in the drawer or on the **Cart** page (`/cart`).
5. Select **Proceed to Checkout**.

## 4. Check out

1. **Customer information**: full legal name, US phone, and email.
2. **Shipping and installation address**: street, optional unit/suite and state/county, city, ZIP/postal code, and optional installation notes (for example preferred technician visit time).
3. **Promo code**: type it in the order summary (for example `WELCOME10` or `FREESHIP`) and select **Apply**. The discount shows before you place your order.
4. **Referral code**: filled in automatically if you registered with one, or enter your consultant's code manually.
5. **Payment method**:

| Method | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Pay Upon Delivery & Installation (COD) | You inspect the unit and pay the certified technician upon delivery and installation. Staff confirm payment afterwards. |
| Hellobee Corporate Bank Wire Transfer | You transfer directly to the Hellobee USA corporate bank account; staff confirm the payment upon receipt. |

6. Select **Place Order**. Totals are calculated and verified by the server.
7. After placing your order, save your **Order Reference** (`#ORD-XXXXXXXX`) shown on the confirmation screen.

## 5. Track your order

Open **Order History** (`/orders` or `/account` -> **Orders & History**). Each order shows its status and timeline:

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Pending | received, waiting for payment or confirmation |
| Confirmed | payment or installation appointment confirmed |
| Processing | being prepared |
| Shipped | on its way, or a technician visit is scheduled |
| Delivered | completed |
| Cancelled | not going ahead |

Payment shows separately as `Unpaid`, `Paid`, or `Refunded`.

## 6. Manage your account

In **My Account** (`/account`) you can edit your profile (name and phone), add/edit/remove shipping addresses and set a default address, view your assigned hydration specialist and sponsor code, and request a password reset email.

## 7. Reviews, events, and the Hellobee Network

- Leave a review on a product page. Reviews appear after admin moderation.
- **Events** (`/events`) lists member workshops and community gatherings.
- **Hellobee Network** (`/hellobee`) explains the consultant program: members earn 10% direct commission on paid referred purchases and progress through ranks `1A–5A` and `LV1–LV10`.

## 8. Help

- Hotline (`+1 (800) 555-4355`) and email (`support@hellobee.com`) are in the page footer.
- Warranty: see the product page; official manufacturer warranty applies per product (`1 year` for emGuarde and Air Purifier, `3–5 years` for LeveLuk water ionizers).
