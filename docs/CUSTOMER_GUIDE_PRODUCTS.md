### What the water types are

An ionizer filters tap water and then splits it electrically into types by pH:

| Type | pH | Typical use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Strong alkaline | 11.5 | cleaning food surfaces; not for drinking |
| Alkaline drinking water | 8.5 to 9.5 | everyday drinking |
| Clean water | 7.0 | neutral; taking medicine, preparing baby formula |
| Beauty water | 6.0 | skin and hair rinse |
| Strong acidic | 2.5 | disinfecting surfaces and hands; not for drinking |

The K8 produces all five. Check your model's manual for which types it offers.

### Installation and first use

1. A certified technician visits to install the unit under or beside the sink (installation is included with ionizer purchases).
2. The unit needs a standard water supply and a power outlet. Power supply is multi-voltage (100 to 240 V) on the K8; power draw is about 230 W while running.
3. After installation the technician runs the first flush and shows you the touchscreen or buttons.
4. Let the first few liters run to waste, as the technician instructs.

### Daily use

1. Select the water type on the screen or keypad.
2. Open the faucet and wait for the voice prompt or indicator before using the water.
3. Drink alkaline water in moderation and as advised on your product's label. This product is not intended to diagnose, cure, or prevent any disease.
4. Run the automatic cleaning cycle when the unit prompts you.

### Care

- Replace the filter on the schedule in your manual.
- Use only genuine cleaning solution supplied or approved by the manufacturer.
- Do not store or serve strong acidic or strong alkaline water for drinking.
- If the unit shows an error code, note it and contact support or your technician.

## 6. Safety notes

- These devices are not a substitute for medical advice. Ask a doctor before changing what you drink if you have a medical condition or take medication.
- Keep electrical parts dry and use only the supplied charging cable.
- Supervise children around water ionizers and strong acidic or alkaline water.
